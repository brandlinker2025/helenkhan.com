Reel 07 · About

“I don’t just take pictures. I keep the feeling of the day.”

Hi, I’m Helena Khan, a passionate photographer and cinematographer based in Dhaka. Over the years I’ve blended my love for storytelling with professional visual arts.

My camera journey started with the biker community, chasing rides and rolling shots, and grew into weddings, birthdays and full brand work for top restaurants. Whether it’s the raw emotion of a wedding, the adrenaline of a group ride, or the entire digital identity and viral videos of a restaurant, I bring the same cinematic vision to every frame.
